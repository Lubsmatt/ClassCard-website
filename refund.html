Refund Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

In short: you have 7 days after we deliver ClassCard to ask for a refund if it does not work for you and we cannot fix it.

1. When you can get a refund

  • The file will not open or run in a current version of Chrome or Edge on your computer, and we could not fix the problem within 3 days of you telling us.
  • ClassCard clearly does not do something our order page says it does.
  • You were charged twice, or you did not receive your download email and we could not resolve it.

2. How to ask

Contact us within 7 days of receiving the file. Tell us your school name, the email you ordered with, and what went wrong. A screenshot helps. We will first try to solve the problem, because most issues can be fixed quickly.

3. When we cannot refund

  • After the 7 days have passed.
  • If the file was shared with other schools or people, which our Terms of Sale do not allow.
  • For problems caused by your printer, an outdated browser, or data you entered or lost.

4. How refunds are paid

We approve or decline a refund request within 3 working days. Approved refunds are returned through Dodo Payments to the method you paid with. Your bank or card provider may take several more days to show it. After a refund, you must stop using ClassCard and delete the file.

5. Contact

Contact us on WhatsApp using the order button on the order page.
