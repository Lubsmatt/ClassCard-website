Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

In short: the ClassCard software does not send your learners' information to us or to anyone else. It stays on your computer. We only keep the details you give us when you order.

1. The ClassCard software

  • ClassCard runs inside your web browser and works offline. Marks, names, photos, comments and settings are saved in your browser on your own computer.
  • We do not receive, see, upload or store this information. We do not use analytics or tracking inside the software.
  • If you turn on the password option, the saved data is encrypted on your computer. We cannot open it or reset the password.
  • Backup files are created on your computer. You decide where they are kept and who can open them.

2. Information we collect when you order

When you order, Dodo Payments collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details at checkout and shares with us your name, email, country and order details. If you contact us for support, we also receive what you choose to tell us, such as your school name or WhatsApp number. We use it to confirm payment, deliver the file, give support, and keep records of sales.

3. Payments

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, which processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We do not see or store your card number or other payment credentials. Dodo Payments also emails you your download link.

4. This website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies of our own. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser may contact Google's servers when the page loads. If you open our live demo, any information you type into it stays in your browser.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with Dodo Payments and the messaging service used for support, or where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them for support, accounting and legal reasons, then delete them.

7. Your learners' information

Your school decides what learner information to enter, and is responsible for handling it lawfully. This includes having permission to use learners' photos and keeping printed reports and backup files safe. Please follow the data protection law that applies to you. In Uganda this includes the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to show you, correct or delete the order information we hold about you. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Contact us on WhatsApp using the order button on the order page.
