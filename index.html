Questions heads of school ask

Do we need internet?

No. Save the file to a computer and open it in Chrome or Edge. It works without a connection.

Can the cards show a passport photo?

Yes. Add a photo for each learner on the Marks page. The Photo ID layout shows it large beside the learner's details, and the Classic and Compact layouts can show a smaller one.

How do we get PDF report cards?

Choose "Download a PDF for each learner (ZIP)" and you get one PDF per learner in a single click, named after the learner and ready to send to parents. You can also download one PDF for the whole class, or print the cards.

Can we use our own subjects and grading scale?

Yes. Subjects, the maximum mark and every grade boundary and remark can be changed in Setup.

Where is our learners' data kept?

In the browser on the computer where you use ClassCard. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn on a password to encrypt it, and save a backup file to move the data to another computer.

Can we use it for several classes?

Yes. Add as many classes as you need, such as P1 to P7 or S1 to S6. Each class has its own learners, subjects and class positions, and you print one class at a time.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Entering marks works on a phone, but a computer is best for printing the cards.
